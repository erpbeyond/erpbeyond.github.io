🏨

Inpatient (IPD)

Real-time bed occupancy, full ADT workflow, nursing rounds and medication administration records, operation theatre scheduling, continuous billing, and discharge/claim management.

Laboratory (LIS)

Order directly from the encounter, barcode sample tracking, critical-value auto-flagging, HL7/ASTM analyzer integration, and branded reports -- coded with LOINC and SNOMED CT.

Radiology -- RIS + PACS

Orders flow straight into modality worklists. DICOM images stored centrally with a web-based viewer for radiologists to read and report from anywhere, with critical-result flagging and tiered archival.

Standards & Interoperability

Clinical & Data Standards

Built-in from the ground up, not bolted on -- so every module speaks the same coded language.

ICD-11

Diagnosis coding across all clinical modules, replacing free-text diagnoses with structured, reportable codes.

WHO ATC / INN

Drug naming and classification using WHO's Anatomical Therapeutic Chemical index -- avoids ambiguity across brand names.

SNOMED CT

Clinical terminology for findings, procedures, and problem lists, enabling decision support and analytics.

LOINC

Universal codes for lab tests and observations, so results are comparable across labs and facilities.

HL7 FHIR

Modern interoperability standard for exchanging records between modules and with external systems (insurers, govt registries).

DICOM

Standard for storing and transmitting medical images -- underpins the PACS/Radiology integration.
