University Core
Admission to Alumni

A complete Student Lifecycle Management System. Built on ERPNext v15 + Frappe Framework, University Core brings admissions, academics, finance, and compliance onto a single platform -- engineered for how Pakistani universities operate, from multi-campus governance to HEC/PEC/NCBC accreditation.

ERPNext v15 Frappe Framework OBE-Ready Multi-Campus
What Sets It Apart

Engineered for Your University

Curriculum Versioning

Students stay on their admission-time curriculum automatically -- no manual tracking as programs evolve year over year.

True Multi-Campus

Different fee & delivery rules per campus, modeled natively as a Campus-Program Offering -- not bolted on as an afterthought.

OBE-Ready

PLO/CLO mapping and assessment traceability built for Washington Accord, PEC and NCBC accreditation from day one.

Included Out-of-the-Box

Everything a Modern University Needs

Admissions & Applicant Tracking

End-to-end applicant pipeline from inquiry to enrollment, with document verification and merit processing.

Student / Faculty / Guardian Portals

Role-based self-service portals for every stakeholder -- grades, attendance, fee status and communication in one place.

Attendance & Assessment Recording

Class-wise attendance and gradebook, feeding directly into transcripts and OBE assessment traceability.

Fee Invoicing on Native Accounting

Credit-hour and fixed fee heads posted straight to the chart of accounts -- no separate reconciliation needed.

Integrated HR, Payroll & Accounting

Faculty and staff management unified with the same financial engine that runs student billing.

Certificates & Report Cards

Auto-generated, branded transcripts, certificates, and report cards straight from student records.

Custom-Engineered for Your University

Feature Summary & Customizations

Program & Curriculum

Program Level, Delivery System, curriculum versioning, structured course codes, and course reuse across curricula.

Fee Management

Credit-hour & fixed fee heads, installment approvals, immutable vouchers with contra-entry corrections, USD billing for foreign students, and multi-currency accounts.

OBE Module

PLO/CLO mapping and assessment traceability for Washington Accord, PEC and NCBC accreditation.

Course Enrollment

Prerequisite & credit-hour validation, with automatic fee adjustment on course add/drop.

Multi-Campus

Campus-Program Offering model -- independent fee structures and delivery rules per campus.
